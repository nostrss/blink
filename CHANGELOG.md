# Changelog

[한국어](CHANGELOG.ko.md)

## 1.4.0 - Unreleased (in App Store review)

- **Typing statistics.** See your keystrokes and keys per minute, by input source and by app. Turn it on in Settings > Statistics. It asks for Input Monitoring permission and counts keys only, never which keys or what you type. It's off until you turn it on.
- **Today at a glance.** The top of the menu shows a summary card for today: switches, typing and pointer use.
- **Pointer badge modes.** Choose when the badge shows: Always, While Moving, or When Clicking. It now appears briefly where you click by default, and hides over the menu bar and Dock.
- **Grouped settings.** The sidebar is grouped by feature: General, Input Source HUD (Display, Position, Appearance), Pointer Badge and Statistics.
- About Blink has a button to turn on typing statistics.
- Menu items no longer have keyboard shortcuts.

If you use the pointer badge, it now shows when you click. Change it in Settings > Pointer Badge > Show Badge.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-10-29

- **Statistics.** See how many times you switched input sources today at the top of the menu. Choose Statistics… for Today, 7 and 30 days, with pointer travel, average speed, clicks and scrolling. On by default.
- Everything stays on your Mac. Blink counts switches and pointer use only, never what you type. Turn it off or clear it in Settings > Statistics.
- **Tidier settings.** Options are grouped into boxes, like System Settings.
- About Blink shows what's new, and opens once after an update.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-10-28

- **Liquid Glass.** On macOS Tahoe 26, the Default theme draws the HUD and the pointer badge with Liquid Glass. Text stays readable over light and dark windows.
- **Classic theme.** Prefer the previous look? Choose Classic in Settings > Appearance.
- With Reduce Transparency on, Blink keeps a solid background.

## 1.1.1 - 2026-10-24

Tagged `v1.1.0` in this repository.

- **Display style.** Show input sources by name or as a short label like "EN" or "한". Hide the previous source to get a tiny one-label badge.
- **Caps Lock HUD.** See "⇪ On" or "⇪ Off" whenever you press Caps Lock.
- **Pointer badge.** Keep the current input source next to your mouse pointer. Pick its side and size.
- Hiding the previous source hides the arrow too.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-23

First release.

- Shows "previous → new" on screen the moment your input source changes, for the blink of an eye.
- Appears on the screen where your mouse is, above full-screen apps and on every Space. Clicks pass straight through.
- Display time from 0.5 to 5 seconds.
- Nine positions, or drag it anywhere.
- Themes: Default, High Contrast, Dracula, Gruvbox, Rosé Pine, or your own colors.
- Size, corners, font weight, and an option to show only the new language.
- Follows Reduce Transparency and Reduce Motion.
- Launch at login, only if you want it.
- English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Russian, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil).
